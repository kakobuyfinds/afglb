What to check before you order

Compare actual measurements

For a hoodie, measure pit to pit and back length on one you already wear. Compare those figures in centimeters with the seller’s chart. For shoes, check whether the chart shows foot length or insole length.

Review QC photos by variant

If the agent provides warehouse photos, match the size label and color against your order. Check close-ups for stitching, stains and visible damage. Review the return deadline before approving shipment.

Compare the cost to your door

Add the item price, domestic delivery, agent fees, international shipping and any applicable tax or duty. Compare shipping quotes for the same destination and check whether the carrier bills by actual or volumetric weight.

OrientDig spreadsheet FAQ

Where do the product links go?

Each item opens its HipobuyFind listing. This independent guide uses the HipobuyFind catalog to help you compare listings before deciding where to order.

What should I do if a listing link stops working?

Search HipobuyFind by the item ID or product title. Before using a replacement listing, compare its photos, variant, size chart and seller details with the item you wanted.

Why can the total differ from the CNY price in the table?

The table shows the price listed in the catalog snapshot. The final amount depends on the current seller price, selected variant, exchange rate, shipping, fees and any applicable taxes.
